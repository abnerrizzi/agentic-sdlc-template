# Code of conduct

Back to the [README](README.md).

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/) by reference. The full text lives at that link.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Critique work, not people. This applies to reviews of agent-authored changes too: the human who opened the MR/PR is the author.
- Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer listed as `@OWNER` in [CODEOWNERS](CODEOWNERS). Reports are handled confidentially.
