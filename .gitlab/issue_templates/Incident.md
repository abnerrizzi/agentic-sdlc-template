## Summary

One paragraph: what happened, in plain words.

## Impact

Who or what was affected, and for how long.

## Timeline

All times in UTC.

- HH:MM - first sign
- HH:MM - detected
- HH:MM - mitigated
- HH:MM - resolved

## Detection

How did we find out? Alert, user report, or luck? Could we have found out sooner?

## Mitigation

What stopped the damage, and what fixed the cause.

## Follow-ups

- [ ] Follow-up action, with an owner
- [ ] Always add a learnings inbox entry (docs/learnings/inbox/)
